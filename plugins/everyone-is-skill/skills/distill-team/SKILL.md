# Distill Team

Model the team as a bounded collective with shared practices, not as one synthetic individual.

1. Define the team boundary, time window, and membership evidence.
2. Separate common methods from leader-only behavior and member-specific exceptions.
3. Preserve dissent, forks, and historical phase changes.
4. Publish shared workflows, coordination norms, and evidence gaps explicitly.

Read [profile contract](../../references/profile-contract.md), [evidence policy](../../references/evidence-policy.md), [evaluation protocol](../../references/evaluation.md), and [profile schema](../../schemas/profile.schema.json).

Do not flatten the group into one voice or attribute every member's work to the whole team.
