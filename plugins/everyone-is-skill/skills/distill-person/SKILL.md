# Distill Person

Extract capability, taste, and decision rules that transfer beyond one anecdote.

1. Bound the target role and intended use.
2. Prefer observed work products, public decisions, and first-person explanations over admirer summaries.
3. Convert repeated moves into explicit triggers, steps, checks, and failure modes.
4. Keep communication style optional and subordinate to method.

Read [profile contract](../../references/profile-contract.md), [evidence policy](../../references/evidence-policy.md), [profile schema](../../schemas/profile.schema.json), and [claim schema](../../schemas/claim.schema.json).

Do not collapse a person into pure persona, private speculation, or ungrounded motivational traits.
