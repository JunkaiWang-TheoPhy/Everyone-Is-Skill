# Method profile

## Method kernel

Describe the smallest reusable scientific method that survives transfer beyond
surface phrasing.

1. State the target problem, invariant, or phenomenon.
2. Identify what evidence would support a person-specific method rather than a
   field-generic move.
3. Separate the operative mechanism from collaborator effects and later
   rationalization.
4. Record what must remain unresolved until stronger evidence appears.

## Selection criteria

- Prefer reusable operations over biography.
- Prefer evidence-backed judgment over stylistic imitation.
- Treat unsupported person-specific claims as abstention cases.

## Output contract

Report the problem class, operative method, evidence threshold,
counterevidence, coauthor risk, and transfer boundary.
