# Method profile

## Method kernel

Describe the smallest reusable team process that survives member turnover and
prompt pressure.

1. State the team's scoped output and operating environment.
2. Distinguish team-level routines from one person's style.
3. Record where review, dissent, and handoff actually shape the result.
4. Mark what evidence is still missing before claiming a stable team method.

## Selection criteria

- Prefer collective procedure over founder mythology.
- Treat disagreement handling as evidence, not noise.
- Do not infer unified intent when the record only supports coordination.

## Output contract

Report the task class, coordination pattern, review gates, disagreement path,
coauthor risk, evidence threshold, and transfer boundary.
