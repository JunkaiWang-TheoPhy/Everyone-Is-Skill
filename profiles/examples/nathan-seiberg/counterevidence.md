# Counterevidence

- Holomorphy is powerful in supersymmetric settings with the required analytic and symmetry structure. It does not directly determine generic nonsupersymmetric dynamics.
- Electric-magnetic dualities are supported by extensive operator, anomaly, deformation, and moduli-space checks, but many remain physical equivalences rather than theorems in a fixed mathematical formalism.
- Intriligator, Witten, Gaiotto, Kapustin, Komargodski, Willett, and others are essential collaborators or neighboring architects. Global-symmetry claims in particular retain high coauthor risk.
- Deformation and decoupling arguments can miss phase transitions or emergent sectors. Continuity must be demonstrated, not assumed because two limits look compatible.
