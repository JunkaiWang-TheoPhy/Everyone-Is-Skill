# Counterevidence

- The early topological-order program, string-net constructions, local-unitary classification, and group-cohomology work involve different collaborators and mathematical tools. Their recurrence supports a broad construction-and-classification pattern, not a single invariant recipe.
- Group cohomology captures important bosonic SPT families but is not a universal classification of all interacting, fermionic, crystalline, gapless, or higher-categorical phases.
- Exactly solvable or tensor-network-friendly lattice models can reveal mechanisms while remaining special points. Robust phase claims still require perturbation and equivalence checks.
- Kitaev and Levin are close matched peers for parts of this profile. A generic instruction to use topology is field-generic and should not be attributed to Wen without the entanglement-first and constructive-classification combination.
