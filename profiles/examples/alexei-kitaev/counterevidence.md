        # Counterevidence

        - An exactly solvable Hamiltonian can still require specially chosen couplings.
- A phase may be robust even if the reference point is finely tuned.
- A minimal model can omit ingredients needed for an actual material or device.
- Coauthored literature does not justify attributing every model move to one person.

        The profile should stay within the documented scope, preserve uncertainty, and refuse to turn a method into a fictional personal replica.
