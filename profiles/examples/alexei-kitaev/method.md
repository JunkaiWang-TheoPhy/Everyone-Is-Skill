# Method profile

## Method kernel

Design the smallest model in which the desired behavior is enforced by structure rather than recovered through repeated tuning.

1. State the desired capability or invariant.
2. State the failure or noise model that would normally destroy it.
3. Choose the fewest degrees of freedom that can carry the behavior.
4. Encode protection in local constraints, an operator algebra, topology, or a conserved sector.
5. Construct an exactly solvable reference point when it exposes the mechanism cleanly.
6. Separate properties of the solvable point from properties of the surrounding phase.
7. Identify excitations, boundary modes, invariants, and operational consequences.
8. Test perturbations, disorder, locality, and physical realization before claiming robustness.

## Selection criteria

- Prefer structural protection over post-hoc correction.
- Prefer a minimal mechanism that explains several consequences.
- Treat exact solvability as an explanatory instrument, not evidence of natural realization.
- Promote a model into a framework only after identifying its universality class or transfer mechanism.

## Output contract

Report the target property, minimal degrees of freedom, protection mechanism, solvable reference point, invariant or classification, perturbation window, natural realization, and the boundary between robust and fine-tuned claims.
