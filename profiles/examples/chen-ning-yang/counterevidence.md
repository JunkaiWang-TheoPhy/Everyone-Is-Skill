# Counterevidence

- Yang-Mills theory was created jointly with Robert Mills, the parity analysis jointly with Tsung-Dao Lee, and the global gauge-field work jointly with Tai-Tsun Wu. The profile must not turn shared results into sole-authorship mythology.
- The historical importance of gauge invariance does not mean symmetry principles determine a unique realistic dynamics; representations, matter content, anomalies, and empirical input remain essential.
- Exact one-dimensional delta-gas and factorized-scattering results rely on special integrability. Their transferable method is to search for consistency and exact structure in a controlled reduction, not to assume generic many-body systems are integrable.
- Later mathematical language of fiber bundles clarified the gauge theory globally. Retrospective geometric interpretation should not be projected anachronistically onto every step of the 1954 construction.
