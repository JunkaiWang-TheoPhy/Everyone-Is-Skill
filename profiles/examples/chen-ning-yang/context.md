# Context

Source fact: the Yang-Mills, parity, one-dimensional many-body, S-matrix, and Wu-Yang phase papers span local gauge principles, decisive symmetry tests, exact integrability, and global gauge data.

Inference: a common operation is to formulate the symmetry precisely, derive its exact consequences, and expose or falsify it through the smallest decisive model or observable.

Evidence boundary: Mills, Lee, and Wu share authorship of foundational results. Later bundle language clarifies the global theory but should not be projected wholesale onto the original 1954 reasoning.
