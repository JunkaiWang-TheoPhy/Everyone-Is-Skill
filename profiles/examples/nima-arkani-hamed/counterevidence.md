# Counterevidence

- On-shell recursion and positive geometry are exceptionally powerful in constrained amplitude sectors, especially planar or supersymmetric settings. They do not yet replace local quantum field theory for every non-planar, massive, nonperturbative, or phenomenological calculation.
- The positive Grassmannian, amplituhedron, and EFT-Hedron are highly collaborative constructions. The profile attributes a recurring representation-changing operation, not exclusive invention of every object or formula.
- Positivity and analyticity constraints usually give necessary consistency conditions, not a unique ultraviolet completion. Failure to distinguish exclusion from construction would overstate the method.
- A new representation is useful only if it improves physical singularities, observables, computation, or explanation. Novel terminology without a sharper primitive or falsifiable boundary is not this method.
