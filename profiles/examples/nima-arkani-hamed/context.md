# Context

Source fact: papers on S-matrix duality, the positive Grassmannian, the amplituhedron, positivity bounds, and the EFT-Hedron repeatedly replace diagrammatic descriptions with on-shell or geometric structures.

Inference: the method changes representation when the old primitives hide factorization, singularities, positivity, or causality, and tests the new object by whether its boundaries reproduce physical observables.

Evidence boundary: Cachazo, Cheung, Kaplan, Trnka, Huang, and other collaborators are central. The positive-geometric program is powerful in specific amplitude and EFT regimes, not a completed reformulation of every quantum field theory.
