# Counterevidence

- AdS/CFT began as a conjecture in controlled brane and large-N limits. Its enormous body of checks does not make every holographic extrapolation a theorem or provide a dual for every quantum field theory.
- Entanglement/geometry connections in eternal AdS black holes and ER=EPR are shared developments with distinct assumptions. They should not be compressed into the claim that entanglement literally produces arbitrary spacetime.
- SYK-like and other minimal systems preserve selected low-energy, chaos, or gravitational features while discarding much of a higher-dimensional theory. A toy model is a controlled representation, not the full object.
- Witten, Susskind, Stanford, Lin, and many others are essential coauthors or neighboring architects. The supported method is the repeated use of controlled limits, explicit dictionaries, and small models, not exclusive ownership of holography.
