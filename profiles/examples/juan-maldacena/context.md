# Context

Source fact: work from the original large-N correspondence through eternal AdS black holes, ER=EPR, SYK, and minimal black-hole systems repeatedly uses controlled limits, dual dictionaries, simple quantum states, and reduced models.

Inference: the transferable method is to find the smallest regime in which both sides of a dual description remain calculable, compare protected observables, and expand only after the dictionary survives.

Evidence boundary: Susskind, Stanford, Lin, and the wider holography community are indispensable. A duality check is not automatically a proof outside its stated limit.
