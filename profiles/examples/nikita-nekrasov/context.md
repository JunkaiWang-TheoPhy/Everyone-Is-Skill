# Context

Source fact: instanton counting, random partitions, the Nekrasov-Shatashvili limit, and BPS/CFT papers deform protected gauge-theory observables into fixed-point sums and exact algebraic identities.

Inference: the recurring operation is to choose a deformation that makes a protected quantity exact, retain its parameters, and recover geometry or integrability through controlled limits.

Evidence boundary: Okounkov, Shatashvili, and the broader localization and BPS/CFT communities are central. Exactness applies to selected observables and chambers, not the undifferentiated full theory.
