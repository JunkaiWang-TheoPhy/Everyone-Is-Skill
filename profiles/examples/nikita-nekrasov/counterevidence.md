# Counterevidence

- Localization computes protected observables in theories with sufficient supersymmetry or equivariance. It does not make every observable or nonsupersymmetric theory exactly solvable.
- The Omega background is a deformation with parameters and limits, not a harmless notation change. Physical interpretation depends on which limit is taken and which quantities remain protected.
- The gauge/integrability and BPS/CFT bridges are shared programs involving Okounkov, Shatashvili, and many others. Claims about the whole correspondence retain collaborator and field-level risk.
- Exact partition functions can encode remarkable geometry while still depending on compactification, contour, chamber, boundary condition, or regularization choices. Those choices must remain visible in any transfer.
