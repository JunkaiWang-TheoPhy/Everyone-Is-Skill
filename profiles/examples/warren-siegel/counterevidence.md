# Counterevidence

- Manifest supersymmetry and covariance can simplify structural reasoning while making component physics, gauge choices, or numerical evaluation less immediate. The best representation depends on the observable.
- Superspace and supergraph methods were developed collectively, especially with Gates, Grisaru, and Rocek. The profile must preserve that high coauthor risk.
- BRST and string-field formulations can introduce ghosts, auxiliary structures, and large state spaces. Formal unification is not automatically computational economy.
- Compact notation is valuable only when definitions, conventions, and recoverable components remain explicit. Compression that hides sign, reality, or gauge conditions is a failure of this method.
