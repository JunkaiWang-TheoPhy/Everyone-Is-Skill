# Counterevidence

- These nine scientists did not form one team, and the methods arose in different fields, institutions, generations, and collaborator networks. The collective is a curatorial comparison only.
- Similar vocabulary can conceal incompatible primitives: rigorous PDE existence, physical duality, exact localization, topological phase equivalence, and positive geometry do not use the same standards of proof.
- Historical sequencing is not a progress ladder. Symmetry, locality, analytic estimates, component formalisms, and direct calculation remain indispensable where later abstractions are unavailable.
- Minority strategies must remain visible. Remove a method from a task only because its assumptions or observables fail, not because another profile is more fashionable or rhetorically unified.
